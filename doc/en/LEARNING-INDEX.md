**didactic (teach → test) → guide to act with euros**.

On the home page, each didactic unit shows the explanation followed directly
by an invitation and link to its corresponding test. The second
part gathers practical rules, the money cycle and euro simulations.

## Activity order

The official test-block sequence is:

1. `concepts-money`
2. `needs-vs-wants`
3. `budget-first`
4. `before-buying`
5. `go-shopping`
6. `change-back`
7. `my-shopping-day`
8. `safe-money`

The integrated `my-shopping-day` activity comes after the partial skills
and adds saving as its fifth round. `safe-money` stays last because it is
the protection skill.

## Simulation order

The third block is presented as:

1. balance;
2. goals and saving;
3. spending in euros;
4. plan a purchase;
5. working out change;
6. safety before sending money;
7. everyday rights and how to complain;
8. clear communication about prices, boundaries and help;
9. safe responses to unexpected problems;
10. purchase cycle: order → delivery note → invoice → payment;
11. depreciation: estimate the value an item keeps;
12. obsolescence: compare repair, reuse and replacement;
13. calculate a hypothetical return;
14. compare return and risk;
15. recognise investment operations;
16. recognise bank cards and accounts;
17. compare renting and owning.

This is the visual order of the cards and the initial guidance. Simulations
are not levels to unlock: they are repeatable tools. The **Your next step**
card suggests the most useful path for the current state: balance → goal →
expense.

The safety, rights, communication and emergency simulations are safe practice: they award no Tokens and do
not write to the ledger. The ledger changes only when a person confirms a
real expense, goal or goal contribution in its corresponding flow.
